# ClimateSafe AI

Open-source climate resilience intelligence infrastructure for vulnerable communities, schools, and farmers.

---

## Overview

ClimateSafe AI is a decentralized climate resilience platform designed to improve:
- climate adaptation,
- public health preparedness,
- food security resilience,
- and early warning systems across vulnerable communities.

The platform combines:
- community-generated climate reports,
- AI-assisted risk analysis,
- school resilience systems,
- agricultural climate intelligence,
- and geospatial vulnerability mapping.

---

## Core Modules

### CommunityWatch
Real-time environmental and climate incident reporting.

### HealthShield
Climate-health monitoring and outbreak intelligence.

### ClimateSafe Schools
School resilience and child-focused preparedness systems.

### FarmGuard AI
Agricultural climate intelligence and food security monitoring.

### AlertNet
Localized climate and emergency alert systems.

### ResilienceMap
Interactive climate vulnerability mapping infrastructure.
